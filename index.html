How to play
  • Deal: Get letter cards and an empty board.
  • Build: Pick cards to make a word. Words must have at least 4 letters.
  • Score: Build a real word from the dictionary to earn points.
  • Smash: Pick letters on the board and smash them.
  • Next round: Click Next round to get new cards.
  • Shop: Spend your points on power-ups at any time during the round.
  • Game over: The game ends when no valid word can be made.
